Featured case study

Where Toronto's subway delay minutes go

I cleaned about 90,000 TTC incident records and asked one operations question: if you could fix only a few things, which would recover the most time?

  • Python · pandas
  • SQL · DuckDB
  • Pareto analysis
  • Data visualization

Customer incidents drive half of all delay. Long incidents drive a fifth.

Delay minutes by cause category

Customer-related incidents (disorderly patrons, people at track level, ill customers) outweigh every other category combined.

01

Staff the hotspots

Put station staff and constables at the ten busiest-delay stations during the 4 to 6 pm peak, where customer incidents concentrate.

02

Cut false-alarm time

Alarms where no trouble was found cost 14,689 minutes. A standard verify-and-release procedure targets that directly.

03

Plan for long incidents

Pre-planned shuttle buses and recovery steps shorten the rare 30+ minute delays that drive a fifth of lost time.
