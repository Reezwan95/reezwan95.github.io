Featured project · Applied ML

Subway Incident Triage

When an incident is logged on Toronto's subway, will it clear in minutes or become a 15+ minute disruption? This model scores each incident on the spot, and an alert rule chosen by cost decides when to act.

74%of major delays caught on unseen 2025 data
−36.5%expected cost vs. "always" or "never" alert
3.2×better than random at ranking risk (PR-AUC 0.30)
33,146delay incidents, 2022–2025, time-based split

Machine learning · Housing policy

Toronto Airbnb: the rules and the prices

29% lower price error than the baseline

All 22,050 Toronto listings from Inside Airbnb. 49% require exactly 28 nights, the first stay length Toronto's short-term rental rules don't cover, and 156 hosts run two or more whole-home short-term listings despite the one-residence rule. A gradient-boosted model prices active listings within $78 a night on average, against $111 for a neighbourhood median.
